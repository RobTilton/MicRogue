## Status And Purpose

This is a non-authoritative preservation document. It retains useful occupant-family ideas discovered before Layout Interpretation pivoted to architecture/site purpose. Nothing here establishes a Production API, approved catalog, required behavior, or implementation authority.

The preserved premise is that occupants are not architecture. Layout Interpretation should first identify what spaces were built for. A later occupant-family pass may interpret those existing areas, layer basic decorations, and selectively replace or scrub decorations to show how current occupants adapted an older place. That layering can give a dungeon both original purpose and later history.

## Preserved Semantic Boundary

Repeated role names do not require family-specific architectural types. A sleeping area occupied by goblins can remain a Sleeping Area whose later decoration, population, condition, or use is goblin-coded.

Candidate occupant-family behavior may eventually include:

- recognizing which existing architectural areas are useful to the occupants;
- applying family-specific decorations or signs of use;
- removing or replacing incompatible decorations;
- repurposing spaces without rewriting their architectural history;
- leaving evidence of earlier use beneath the current occupation layer.

This later pass must not be assumed to own original architectural interpretation. Its actual inputs, outputs, mutation rights, ordering, and catalog remain undecided.
